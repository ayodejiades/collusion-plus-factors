# Deviations from the Study 2 preregistration (tag prereg2-v1)

## D1: Study 2 was run before the sprint, not during it (18 October 2026)
The preregistration said Study 2 would run during the sprint (from 23 October). At the owner's
decision, to keep the sprint weekend's workload small, it was launched on 18 October 2026, right
after the preregistration was frozen and before any Study 2 market existed. Nothing else changed:
conditions, seeds, thresholds, judges, tests and decision rules are exactly as tagged. The paper's
"What is new this weekend" section reports this date.
